# 🗳 Governance Models with Due Diligence

## 1. Knowledge-Weighted Governance (Your Idea)
- **Core concept**: Knowledge = power.  
- Members earn a **Knowledge Score** by doing due diligence (e.g., reading, reviewing, or researching projects).  
- Score is recorded via:
  - **Soulbound NFT** (non-transferable badge of knowledge).  
  - **Reputation score** tied to wallet.  
- The more projects a member reviews, the more governance influence they earn.  

👉 This ensures **active, informed members** have more power than passive or wealthy ones.  

---

## 2. Quadratic Voting
- **Votes grow by the square root** of tokens/points held.  
- Example:
  - 1 token = 1 vote  
  - 4 tokens = 2 votes  
  - 9 tokens = 3 votes  
- Prevents whales from dominating by making extra votes increasingly costly.  

👉 In your system: combine **Quadratic Voting with Knowledge Score**, so active learners get outsized influence vs. pure token holders.  

---

## 3. Delegated Voting
- Members can **delegate votes** to trusted community representatives.  
- Useful if:
  - Some members lack time or expertise.  
  - They want experts/researchers to represent them.  

👉 Perfect fit with your model: **delegation naturally boosts diligent members**, since others will trust them with votes.  

---

## 4. Multi-House Governance
- Inspired by **bicameral parliaments** (two chambers).  
- Two separate groups must both approve proposals:  
  - **House of Tokens** → represents treasury contributors/investors.  
  - **House of Knowledge** → represents members with due-diligence NFTs/reputation.  
- A proposal passes only if **both houses approve**.  

👉 Benefits:  
- Prevents domination by money **alone**.  
- Prevents domination by knowledge/reputation **alone**.  
- Balances **capital + expertise**.  

---

## 🔑 Combined Model for Your Project
- **Base layer**: Knowledge-Weighted Governance (due diligence NFTs).  
- **Modifier**: Quadratic Voting (reduce whale dominance).  
- **Optional**: Delegation (let members back trusted researchers).  
- **Structure**: Multi-House (balance investors & researchers).  

This creates a **DAO where learning, research, and fairness are rewarded**, while still keeping investors engaged.
